# Edge Discovery Lab

The Edge Discovery Lab is adjacent to, but distinct from, the operator-driven
hypothesis lab.

Its purpose is to develop unconventional, mechanism-led trading ideas through
small joint LLM/operator slices. The default workflow is deliberately slower
than a conventional automated research pipeline:

1. state the proposed mechanism;
2. establish whether the required data are accessible;
3. freeze one small discovery surface;
4. create operator-facing visuals and representative tapes;
5. discuss what the evidence appears to say; and
6. open statistics, controls, wider atlases, or OOS only after an explicit
   operator decision.

The lab does not treat novelty, low capacity, or visual appeal as evidence of
edge. Initial packets are learning artifacts, not strategy candidates.
