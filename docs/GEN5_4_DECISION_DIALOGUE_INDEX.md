## Compact Decision Records

### D01 — Conditional high-beta continuation

- **Question:** What economic mechanism could make information available after
  today's close relevant to the next executable holding interval?
- **Decision:** Test whether asset leadership persists when peer, sector,
  breadth, participation, and broad-market conditions provide independent
  confirmation.
- **Why:** A supervised model cannot create information absent from its inputs;
  the economic mechanism must precede feature or model choice.
- **Deferred:** Generic return prediction, broad feature expansion, and another
  model-parameter search.

### D02 — Conditional exposure quality and benchmarks

- **Question:** How should success be judged when the system sometimes holds
  cash?
- **Decision:** Ask whether the policy assigns more exposure before favorable
  basket intervals and less before unfavorable intervals.
- **Why:** Raw return alone confounds selection, market direction, and time
  invested.
- **Comparators:** cash/no-trade, continuously invested equal-weight basket,
  exposure-matched equal-weight basket, and external market/sector context.

### D03 — Fixed binary sleeves

- **Question:** How can the first POC vary exposure without introducing opaque
  sizing or optimization?
- **Decision:** Give each eligible asset one fixed equal-capital sleeve with a
  binary `0/1` state. Cash from inactive sleeves stays cash.
- **Why:** Aggregate exposure varies transparently while selection remains
  separate from concentration and sizing effects.
- **Deferred:** continuous probability weights, volatility targeting, risk
  parity, Kelly sizing, leverage, and optimization.

### D04 — Execution-aligned outcome and return conventions

- **Question:** Which outcome corresponds to a decision made after the close and
  implemented manually at the next open?
- **Decision:** Observe through close `t`, execute at open `t + 1`, and measure
  the primary interval from open `t + 1` to open `t + 2`.
- **Why:** A label should represent the exposure that could actually have been
  held.
- **Return convention:** simple returns for P&L and portfolio aggregation; log
  returns where time additivity helps feature construction.

### D05 — Six information roles

- **Question:** How do we move beyond indicator monoculture without adding
  uncontrolled feature breadth?
- **Decision:** Start with target leadership, sector confirmation,
  cross-sectional breadth, broad-market trend, broad-market volatility, and
  participation/liquidity.
- **Why:** Each role must add economically distinct information rather than
  another transform of the same target-price path.
- **Open:** exact formulas, horizons, panels, and missing-data rules.

### D06 — Comparator legitimacy

- **Question:** What should count as a legitimate peer or sector confirmation?
- **Decision:** Use leave-one-out active-basket leadership as the common core.
  Treat `SMH` as a separate semiconductor challenger for `AMD`, `NVDA`, and
  `AVGO`; do not force a sector column onto `TSLA` or `MSTR`.
- **Why:** Missing economic structure is more honest than a convenient but false
  comparison.

### D07 — Falsification before complexity

- **Question:** What evidence would tell us to stop before fitting more complex
  models?
- **Decision:** Stop if exposure does not separate favorable from unfavorable
  intervals, value-add disappears versus exposure-matched equal weight, results
  concentrate in one symbol/year, effects vanish at executable timing, signs
  change across folds, or costs/search breadth erase the distinction.
- **Why:** A hypothesis that cannot fail clearly becomes an invitation to tune.

### D08 — Auditable semi-manual advice concept

- **Question:** Can rational variable exposure remain compatible with manual
  execution?
- **Decision:** A future advice packet may show current position, target sleeve,
  target dollars/shares, order delta, reason, `as_of_timestamp`, frozen
  authority, and data health.
- **Why:** Computational sizing and auditability do not require automated order
  placement.
- **Boundary:** This is an output concept only; live-advice behavior and
  execution remain closed gates.

### D09 — Retrospective research basket and claim boundary

- **Question:** Can historically exceptional assets support a useful mechanism
  study even though their selection uses future knowledge?
- **Decision:** Freeze `AMD`, `NVDA`, `TSLA`, `MSTR`, and `AVGO` as a deliberately
  retrospective research basket with leave-one-out peers.
- **Why:** The basket supplies a bounded high-beta testbed while making the
  selection bias explicit.
- **Boundary:** Results may describe conditional exposure inside this basket;
  they may not claim prospective asset discovery or broad equity generality.

### D10 — Frozen minimal feature panel

- **Question:** What is the smallest precisely timestamped panel that represents
  the six accepted economic roles without horizon search or false breadth?
- **Decision:** Use 20-session target-minus-peer leadership, leave-one-out
  opportunity-set breadth versus `SPY`, 20-session `SPY` trend and volatility,
  a robust 5-session versus preceding-60-session target dollar-volume ratio,
  and `SMH`-minus-`SPY` confirmation only in a semiconductor challenger.
- **Why:** One shared horizon and one formula per role constrain multiple
  testing while keeping every primitive interpretable.
- **Missingness:** Require aligned complete common inputs; do not impute a
  neutral sector value for `TSLA` or `MSTR`.

### D11 — Frozen primitive validation protocol

- **Question:** How can the first evidence packet test the primitives without
  allowing OOS results to choose folds, bins, costs, or promotion rules?
- **Decision:** Use `2020Q1`-`2024Q4` quarterly OOS authorities, eight preceding
  TRAIN quarters, TRAIN-defined diagnostic quintiles, target h1 outcomes,
  separate basket context, and fixed `10`/`20` basis-point one-way cost cases.
- **Promotion:** Require positive separation and ordering in at least `12 / 20`
  folds, limited symbol-year concentration, base-cost value-add, and no
  stress-cost reversal. Both leadership and opportunity breadth must pass.
- **Boundary:** The packet fits no model and selects no trading threshold. A
  fixed top-two-quintile proxy exists only to expose turnover and cost burden.

### D12 — Primitive POC readout and STOP

- **Question:** Do the frozen primitives jointly support the conditional
  high-beta continuation mechanism strongly enough to permit model fitting?
- **Decision:** No. Target leadership and dollar-volume participation passed
  their individual gates, but required opportunity-set breadth failed its
  separation, ordering, and cost gates.
- **Why:** The protocol required both leadership and breadth to pass before a
  model comparison. Dropping the failed breadth requirement after seeing OOS
  evidence would convert falsification into post hoc feature selection.
- **Boundary:** No GLM, XGBoost, probability threshold, allocation, or live
  behavior is authorized from this packet.

### D13 — Leadership x participation confirmation

- **Question:** Does target leadership persist specifically when abnormal
  participation accompanies it?
- **Decision:** Freeze a 2x2 leadership/high-low x participation/high-low state
  table using pooled TRAIN 60th-percentile thresholds and confirm only on
  `2025Q1`-`2026Q2`.
- **Falsifier:** State A must beat both high-leadership/low-participation State B
  and low-leadership/high-participation State C in at least `4 / 6` quarters,
  with positive pooled contrasts and cost robustness.
- **Why:** This tests whether participation adds information conditional on
  leadership rather than merely reusing two individually attractive OOS
  features.
- **Boundary:** The confirmation is a state diagnostic with human-facing
  visuals, not a fitted model or selected trading policy.

### D14 — Leadership × participation confirmation STOP

- **Question:** Did the separately frozen high-leadership/high-participation
  state add information beyond leadership alone on the fresh confirmation
  window?
- **Decision:** No. Stop this interaction before model fitting.
- **Why:** State A beat both single-condition comparators in only `2 / 6`
  quarters. Pooled A−B was `-15.7 bp`, and selection-excess was negative at both
  the `10 bp` base cost and `20 bp` stress cost.
- **Qualification:** Pooled A−C was positive and concentration stayed below the
  frozen cap, but those passes cannot override the failed ordering, A−B, and
  cost gates.
- **Boundary:** Do not retune the quantile, horizon, or interaction on this OOS
  evidence. No model, allocation, or live behavior is authorized.

## Current Stop State

Both bounded market-only tests returned `STOP`. The primitive packet found that
leadership and participation passed individually but required opportunity-set
breadth failed. The fresh leadership × participation confirmation then found
that the joint state beat both single-condition comparators in only `2 / 6`
quarters and failed the cost gates. Model fitting, threshold search, allocation,
and live-advice code remain closed. The next step is a theory-first decision
about one genuinely distinct, point-in-time information family—not an OOS-driven
feature deletion or another nearby OHLCV interaction.
