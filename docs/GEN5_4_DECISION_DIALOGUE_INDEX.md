## Compact Decision Records

### D01 — Conditional high-beta continuation

- **Question:** What economic mechanism could make information available after
  today's close relevant to the next executable holding interval?
- **Decision:** Test whether asset leadership persists when peer, sector,
  breadth, participation, and broad-market conditions provide independent
  confirmation.
- **Why:** A supervised model cannot create information absent from its inputs;
  the economic mechanism must precede feature or model choice.
- **Deferred:** Generic return prediction, broad feature expansion, and another
  model-parameter search.

### D02 — Conditional exposure quality and benchmarks

- **Question:** How should success be judged when the system sometimes holds
  cash?
- **Decision:** Ask whether the policy assigns more exposure before favorable
  basket intervals and less before unfavorable intervals.
- **Why:** Raw return alone confounds selection, market direction, and time
  invested.
- **Comparators:** cash/no-trade, continuously invested equal-weight basket,
  exposure-matched equal-weight basket, and external market/sector context.

### D03 — Fixed binary sleeves

- **Question:** How can the first POC vary exposure without introducing opaque
  sizing or optimization?
- **Decision:** Give each eligible asset one fixed equal-capital sleeve with a
  binary `0/1` state. Cash from inactive sleeves stays cash.
- **Why:** Aggregate exposure varies transparently while selection remains
  separate from concentration and sizing effects.
- **Deferred:** continuous probability weights, volatility targeting, risk
  parity, Kelly sizing, leverage, and optimization.

### D04 — Execution-aligned outcome and return conventions

- **Question:** Which outcome corresponds to a decision made after the close and
  implemented manually at the next open?
- **Decision:** Observe through close `t`, execute at open `t + 1`, and measure
  the primary interval from open `t + 1` to open `t + 2`.
- **Why:** A label should represent the exposure that could actually have been
  held.
- **Return convention:** simple returns for P&L and portfolio aggregation; log
  returns where time additivity helps feature construction.

### D05 — Six information roles

- **Question:** How do we move beyond indicator monoculture without adding
  uncontrolled feature breadth?
- **Decision:** Start with target leadership, sector confirmation,
  cross-sectional breadth, broad-market trend, broad-market volatility, and
  participation/liquidity.
- **Why:** Each role must add economically distinct information rather than
  another transform of the same target-price path.
- **Open:** exact formulas, horizons, panels, and missing-data rules.

### D06 — Comparator legitimacy

- **Question:** What should count as a legitimate peer or sector confirmation?
- **Decision:** Use leave-one-out active-basket leadership as the common core.
  Treat `SMH` as a separate semiconductor challenger for `AMD`, `NVDA`, and
  `AVGO`; do not force a sector column onto `TSLA` or `MSTR`.
- **Why:** Missing economic structure is more honest than a convenient but false
  comparison.

### D07 — Falsification before complexity

- **Question:** What evidence would tell us to stop before fitting more complex
  models?
- **Decision:** Stop if exposure does not separate favorable from unfavorable
  intervals, value-add disappears versus exposure-matched equal weight, results
  concentrate in one symbol/year, effects vanish at executable timing, signs
  change across folds, or costs/search breadth erase the distinction.
- **Why:** A hypothesis that cannot fail clearly becomes an invitation to tune.

### D08 — Auditable semi-manual advice concept

- **Question:** Can rational variable exposure remain compatible with manual
  execution?
- **Decision:** A future advice packet may show current position, target sleeve,
  target dollars/shares, order delta, reason, `as_of_timestamp`, frozen
  authority, and data health.
- **Why:** Computational sizing and auditability do not require automated order
  placement.
- **Boundary:** This is an output concept only; live-advice behavior and
  execution remain closed gates.

### D09 — Retrospective research basket and claim boundary

- **Question:** Can historically exceptional assets support a useful mechanism
  study even though their selection uses future knowledge?
