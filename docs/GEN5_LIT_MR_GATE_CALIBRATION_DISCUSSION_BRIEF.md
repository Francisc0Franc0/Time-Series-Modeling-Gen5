# Literature-Grounded Mean-Reversion Gate Calibration Discussion Brief

## Status

`DISCUSSION_ONLY_NO_GATE_CHANGE`

This brief records the gate-calibration question after the pair and triplet
atlases. It does not revise a contract, reopen a stopped batch, unseal
CONFIRMATION, or authorize another run.

## Evidence Now Available

- The unchanged pair template produced no full pass in the canonical TRAIN
  example, Panels A and B, or Relationship Atlas 01.
- The original eight-triplet batch produced no full pass.
- Triplet Atlas 01 produced one strict TRAIN pass among 28 frozen candidates.
- The nominated `EWA-EWC-EWZ` vector remained positive in one genuine
  2021-2023 OOS replay at the primary 5 bp turnover cost, but became negative
  at the 10 bp stress cost.

The gate question is therefore not simply "nothing ever passes." A strict
triplet conjunction produced a valid nomination and a modest positive OOS
result. Its main weakness is economic margin after higher costs.

## Recommended Posture

Do not relax either strategy's gates yet.

1. Relaxing the pair gates after repeated broad failures would risk rescuing
   the inspected mechanism rather than learning something new.
2. Relaxing the triplet gates is unnecessary to demonstrate throughput or
   produce an OOS candidate; the strict contract already did both.
3. The triplet OOS result is cost-fragile. Lowering statistical admission
   standards does not solve low economic margin.
4. Pair and triplet gates should not be loosened as one global bundle. Their
   structural assumptions differ.

## Gate Roles

### Hard Structural Gates

These protect interpretability, chronology, or the claimed mechanism and
should remain conjunctive:

- integrity and next-open timing;
- complete bounded data coverage;
- triplet \(I(1)\) admissibility;
- exact rank one for the current minimal triplet concept;
- positive-beta semantics for the current pair portfolio;
- frozen-vector identity across OOS; and
- minimum two-sided participation sufficient to estimate both directions.

Dropping exact rank one would not be a minor relaxation. It would open a
different multi-relation portfolio-selection problem and should be named
`LIT-MR-03.2` or a new concept.

### Economic Evidence Gates

These are conservative but directly relevant:

- positive cost-aware mean return with a bootstrap lower bound above zero;
- robust negative forward-convergence evidence;
- pair stability across TRAIN years; and
- matched-random-sign separation for pairs.

The current lower-confidence-bound rules deliberately trade false negatives
for fewer chance nominations in a many-candidate atlas.

### Supporting Diagnostics

These may be candidates for a future graded evidence framework rather than
binary vetoes:

- hit rate above 50%, because positive expectancy can coexist with a lower hit
  rate when payoff asymmetry is favorable;
- an exact annual-count threshold when trade opportunities are uneven across
  years; and
- a fixed vector-cosine threshold near its boundary, provided sign,
  concentration, and exposure drift are reported explicitly.

Moving a diagnostic from veto to supporting evidence would still be a
substantive contract change.
